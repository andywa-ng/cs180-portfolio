Project 0: Becoming Friends with Your Camera

The goal of this project is to gain an intuitive understanding of the relationship between perspective, focal length/zoom, and the center of projection.

Part 1: Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

A close-up selfie often results in distortion. Stepping back and zooming in to match the subject's size helps reduce this distortion and produces a more natural portrait due to the change in perspective.

Part 2: Architectural Perspective Compression

For an architectural scene, zooming in from far away flattens the image compared to standing close and taking a photo without zoom. This demonstrates perspective compression.

[Image: Building Zoomed In (Flattened)]

Far away, zoomed in (Compressed)

[Image: Building Close Up (Uncompressed)]

Part 3: The Dolly Zoom

The Dolly Zoom (or "Vertigo shot") is created by simultaneously moving the camera back while zooming in, keeping the subject the same size in the frame. This creates a disorienting effect where the background appears to warp.
